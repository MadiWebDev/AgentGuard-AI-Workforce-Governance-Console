# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in AgentGuard a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Positive behaviour includes using welcoming and inclusive language, being respectful of differing viewpoints, giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully, and focusing on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, personal or political attacks, publishing others' private information, and any other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that violate this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behaviour they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

To report an issue, contact the maintainers privately — do not open a public issue about a conduct concern. All reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and confidentially.
